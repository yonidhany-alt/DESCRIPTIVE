Describe the World
in English

Learn descriptive text – structure, language, and write your own paragraph. Fun and interactive for Grade 7!

Start Learning

Learning Objectives

Understand

Students understand what descriptive text is and its purpose.

Identify Structure

Recognize generic structure: identification & description.

Language Features

Use adjectives, simple present tense, and specific nouns.

Write

Write a simple descriptive paragraph about a person or place.

Learning Material

Generic Structure

Identification: introduces the subject.
Description: details appearance, qualities, etc.

Language Features

• Adjectives (beautiful, tall)
• Simple present tense
• Specific nouns & adverbs

Example Text

My Cat
I have a pet cat named Mochi. She is small and fluffy. Her fur is white with black spots. She has big green eyes. Mochi likes to sleep on my bed. She is very playful and friendly.

Vocabulary List

fluffyplayfulfriendly beautifultallsmall brightcurious
Important: Use adjectives to describe noun + simple present.
